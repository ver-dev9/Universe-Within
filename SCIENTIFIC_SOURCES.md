# SCIENTIFIC SOURCES & VERIFICATION — UNIVERSE WITHIN

Every astronomical measurement, planetary atmospheric breakdown, physical parameter, and discovery documented in **UNIVERSE WITHIN** is verified against authoritative space agencies and peer-reviewed astrophysical archives.

## 1. Primary Institutional Sources

### 1. NASA Solar System Exploration
- **URL:** [https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system)
- **Verified Data:**
  - Planetary mean diameters, masses, and surface gravity metrics.
  - Orbital semimajor axes, orbital eccentricities, and sidereal rotation periods.
  - Planetary atmospheric volumetric gas breakdowns (percentage ratios for N2, O2, CO2, H2, He, CH4).
  - Current known moon counts (including recent Jovian and Saturnian confirmations through 2024–2026).

### 2. NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Horizons Ephemeris
- **URL:** [https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons)
- **Verified Data:**
  - In-situ distance and velocity telemetry for Voyager 1 and Voyager 2.
  - Main Asteroid Belt dimensional boundaries and Kirkwood gap locations.

### 3. Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI)
- **URL:** [https://www.stsci.edu](https://www.stsci.edu)
- **Verified Data:**
  - James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) NIRCam and MIRI discoveries, including early high-redshift galaxies (JADES-GS-z14-0).
  - Hubble Space Telescope deep field galaxy count estimates.

### 4. Caltech / MIT Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory (LIGO)
- **URL:** [https://www.ligo.caltech.edu](https://www.ligo.caltech.edu)
- **Verified Data:**
  - Direct gravitational wave detection events (GW150914, binary black hole merger).
  - Multi-messenger neutron star kilonova (GW170817) r-process heavy element nucleosynthesis.

### 5. NASA Exoplanet Archive (NExScI)
- **URL:** [https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu)
- **Verified Data:**
  - Confirmed extrasolar planet census numbers (>5,600 confirmed).
  - TRAPPIST-1 system habitability parameters.

## 2. Distinction Between Scientific Consensus & Hypotheses
In accordance with professional scientific communication standards:
- **Established Findings:** Earth habitability feedback loops, Schwarzschild event horizon mathematics, plate tectonics, planetary orbits, and atmospheric composition.
- **Active Ongoing Hypotheses:** Exact particle nature of Dark Matter (WIMPs vs Axions), dynamical equations of Dark Energy (cosmological constant $\Lambda$ vs quintessence), and early Mars paleoclimate models.
